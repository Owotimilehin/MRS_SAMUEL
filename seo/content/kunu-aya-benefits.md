Kunu aya is a creamy drink made from tigernuts, often blended with dates, coconut and ginger. Its main benefits: it is naturally dairy-free, it gives you steady energy, and tigernuts bring fibre, resistant starch and minerals such as magnesium and potassium. Early research suggests it may support gut health, but studies are still small.

Long before "plant milk" became a supermarket shelf, northern Nigeria was drinking kunu aya out of chilled bottles on every street corner. Here is what is in it, what the research says, and how to enjoy it safely in Lagos heat.

## What is kunu aya?

Kunu aya (also called kunun aya or tigernut milk) is a drink from northern Nigeria made by soaking tigernuts, blending them with water and straining out the pulp. Many recipes add **dates** for sweetness, **coconut** for creaminess and **ginger** for a little heat.

Despite the name, a tigernut is not a nut. It is a small tuber, the root of a plant called Cyperus esculentus. In Spain, the same drink is called **horchata de chufa**, and in Valencia it is as everyday as kunu is in Kano.

## What tigernuts bring to the glass

A 2022 review in the journal Foods ([Yu and colleagues](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8871521/)) describes tigernuts as rich in starch, healthy fats, fibre, some protein, and minerals. The fat is mostly oleic acid, the same kind found in olive oil.

How much of that ends up in your glass depends on the recipe. Straining removes much of the fibre, and dates add sugar.

## 6 benefits of kunu aya

**1. Fibre that may help digestion**

Whole tigernuts are high in fibre. Fibre helps keep you regular and feeds the friendly bacteria in your gut, as the [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/carbohydrates/fiber/) explains. A drink that keeps some of the fine pulp gives you more of this than a heavily strained one.

**2. Resistant starch and gut bacteria**

Tigernuts contain resistant starch, a kind of starch that reaches the large intestine and feeds gut bacteria. In a small [2022 pilot study in Spain](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9104503/), healthy adults drank unsweetened tigernut drink daily for three days. Some volunteers showed more of the bacteria linked to butyrate, a compound that helps keep the gut lining healthy. It was a small, short study, so treat this as promising, not proven.

**3. Steady energy**

Starch, natural sugars and fats make kunu aya filling and energising. That is why it has long been a favourite across the North. It is a good mid-morning drink when you are stuck in traffic between Lekki and the Island.

**4. A dairy-free alternative to milk**

Kunu aya contains no milk, so it suits people who avoid dairy. Many adults have trouble digesting lactose, the sugar in milk, according to the [NIH's NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/lactose-intolerance). Kunu aya can stand in for milk in cereal, smoothies or oats. Note it has much less protein than cow's milk.

**5. Minerals such as magnesium and potassium**

Tigernuts contain magnesium and potassium. Your body uses [magnesium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-Consumer/) for muscles, nerves and energy, and [potassium](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Potassium-Consumer/) helps your heart, muscles and nerves work normally (NIH Office of Dietary Supplements). A glass contributes some, but it will not replace a varied diet.

**6. It keeps you full**

Fibre and fat slow digestion, so a glass of kunu aya can keep hunger away for longer than a soft drink. That makes it a better choice than a sugary fizzy drink when you need something to hold you till lunch.

A note on the big claims you see online: fertility, libido and blood sugar claims for tigernut are common on Nigerian social media, but solid human studies are lacking. We do not make those claims.

## Kunu aya vs tigernut milk vs kunu zaki

- **Kunu aya** is tigernut blended with water and strained, often with dates, coconut and ginger. Creamy and sweet.
- **Tigernut milk** is the same idea. The name is just more common in shops and outside Nigeria, and it may be sold plain without dates.
- **Kunu zaki** is a different drink: it is made from grains such as millet or sorghum, spiced with ginger and cloves, and is thinner and grainier.

So if you want the creamy one, ask for kunu aya.

## Who should be careful

- **People watching sugar or calories.** Dates add natural sugar and tigernuts add fat. Even natural sugars count. The [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/news/item/04-03-2015-who-calls-on-countries-to-reduce-sugars-intake-among-adults-and-children) advises limiting free sugars. If you have diabetes, keep portions small and talk to your doctor.
- **People with allergies.** Tigernut is a tuber, not a tree nut, and allergy to it is rare. But recipes may include coconut or be made near nuts. If you have a serious food allergy, ask before you drink.
- **People with sensitive stomachs.** Lots of fibre at once can cause bloating. Start with a small glass.

If you have a medical condition or take regular medication, talk to your doctor before making kunu aya a daily habit.

## How to make kunu aya at home in 6 steps

1. Wash a cup of dried tigernuts well and soak them in clean water for 24 to 48 hours in the fridge.
2. Drain and rinse. Soak a handful of pitted dates for an hour.
3. Blend the tigernuts, dates, a piece of fresh coconut and a small knob of ginger with about four cups of clean water.
4. Strain through a clean cloth or fine sieve, squeezing out the milk.
5. Chill straight away.
6. Drink within a day or two, and shake before you pour.

Use clean, safe water and a clean cloth. Kunu aya spoils quickly when it sits warm.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the benefits of kunu aya?

Kunu aya is dairy-free, filling and gives steady energy. Tigernuts bring fibre, resistant starch, healthy fats and minerals like magnesium and potassium. A small study suggests tigernut drink may support helpful gut bacteria, but more research is needed.

### Is kunu aya the same as tigernut milk?

Yes, mostly. Kunu aya is the Nigerian name for tigernut milk, and it often includes dates, coconut and ginger. Shop-bought tigernut milk may be plain.

### Is kunu aya good for digestion?

It may help. Tigernuts contain fibre and resistant starch, which feed gut bacteria. Start with a small glass if you are not used to much fibre, as it can cause bloating.

### Does kunu aya have a lot of sugar?

It depends on the recipe. Tigernuts are naturally a little sweet and dates add more natural sugar. If you are watching your sugar or have diabetes, keep portions small and ask your doctor.

### How long does kunu aya last?

Fresh kunu aya with no preservatives should be kept cold and drunk within a day or two. Throw it away if it smells sour or fizzy. Never leave it in the sun or a hot car.
