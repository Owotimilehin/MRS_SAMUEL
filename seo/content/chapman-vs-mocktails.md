Chapman is Nigeria's classic party drink: orange and lemon-lime soda with Angostura bitters, grenadine or blackcurrant syrup, cucumber and citrus slices. A mocktail is any alcohol-free mixed drink, and can be built on fresh juice instead of soda. Chapman contains a small amount of alcohol from the bitters; a fresh-juice mocktail can be fully alcohol-free and lower in added sugar.

At every Lagos wedding, naming ceremony and Owambe, someone asks the bartender for chapman. It is red, it is fizzy, it has cucumber floating on top, and it tastes like every childhood party you remember. But more and more hosts are also asking for "mocktails". So which should you serve? Here is a clear comparison, with recipes for both.

## What is chapman?

Chapman is a soda-based fruit punch that has been a Nigerian favourite for decades. Its exact origin story is debated, and you will hear different versions involving colonial-era clubs and a bartender or member named Chapman. What everyone agrees on is the taste: sweet, fizzy, fruity and slightly bitter.

A typical Nigerian chapman includes:

- **Orange soda** (often Fanta)
- **Lemon-lime soda** (often Sprite or 7Up)
- **Angostura aromatic bitters**, a few dashes
- **Grenadine or blackcurrant cordial** for the red colour
- **Cucumber slices**
- **Orange and lemon slices**
- **Lots of ice**

Some people add a splash of fresh lime, a few mint leaves or a little red wine for a grown-up version.

## Does chapman contain alcohol?

This question comes up all the time, especially from Muslim guests, pregnant women and parents.

Angostura aromatic bitters is made with a high-strength spirit base, so the bottle itself is strongly alcoholic. In a chapman, only a few dashes go into a large glass of soda, so the final drink contains a very small amount of alcohol. It is not "zero".

If a guest avoids alcohol completely, for religious reasons, pregnancy, medication or personal choice, a classic chapman is not the right drink for them. Leave out the bitters, or serve a fresh-juice mocktail instead. If you use a red wine "chapman", that is a cocktail, not a mocktail.

## What is a mocktail?

A mocktail is any mixed drink made without alcohol. It borrows the look and fun of a cocktail: nice glass, ice, garnish, layered colours. Mocktails can be built on soda, juice, coconut water, tea, sparkling water or a mix.

Strictly speaking, chapman without bitters is a mocktail. But when Lagos hosts ask for mocktails today, they usually mean something fresher: fruit juice, herbs, sparkling water and very little syrup.

## Chapman vs mocktails: side by side

**Alcohol**

- **Chapman:** a small amount, from the bitters.
- **Fresh-juice mocktail:** none, if you skip bitters and spirits.

**Sugar**

- **Chapman:** two sodas plus grenadine or cordial means a lot of added sugar per glass.
- **Fresh-juice mocktail:** the sugar comes mainly from the fruit. Fruit juice sugars still count as "free sugars" according to the [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/news/item/04-03-2015-who-calls-on-countries-to-reduce-sugars-intake-among-adults-and-children), so portions still matter, but there is no need to add syrup.

**Taste**

- **Chapman:** sweet, bitter-edged and fizzy. Nostalgic.
- **Fresh-juice mocktail:** fruitier and fresher. Can be tart, spicy (with ginger) or creamy.

**Colour and look**

- Both can look stunning. Chapman's classic red comes from grenadine; a mocktail can get the same colour from beetroot, watermelon or zobo.

**Cost and effort**

- **Chapman:** cheap and quick to batch with supermarket sodas.
- **Mocktail:** a little more prep if you press your own juice, or easy if you buy fresh cold-pressed juice and just add ice and sparkling water.

**Who it suits**

- **Chapman:** guests who love the classic and are fine with a little alcohol and sugar.
- **Mocktail:** mixed crowds, Muslim guests, children, pregnant guests, people watching sugar, and anyone driving home on Third Mainland Bridge at midnight.

The honest answer for most parties is: serve both. Label them clearly.

## How to make classic Nigerian chapman

Makes one large glass.

1. Fill a tall glass with ice.
2. Add 150ml orange soda and 150ml lemon-lime soda.
3. Add 2 to 3 dashes of Angostura bitters.
4. Stir in 1 to 2 tablespoons of grenadine or blackcurrant cordial.
5. Add cucumber slices, an orange slice and a lemon slice.
6. Stir gently and serve straight away.

**For a no-alcohol version:** skip the bitters and add a squeeze of fresh lime and a few mint leaves for complexity.

## Tips for serving drinks at a Lagos party

- **Keep everything cold.** Fresh juice has no preservatives. Plan fridge or cooler space before delivery day, and keep bottles out of the sun.
- **Add ice at the last minute.** Pre-mixed drinks with ice go watery fast in Lagos heat.
- **Add the fizz just before serving.** Sparkling water and soda go flat in a jug.
- **Label the bitters.** Put a small card on the chapman: "contains Angostura bitters (alcohol)". Your Muslim and pregnant guests will thank you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does chapman contain alcohol?

Classic chapman contains a small amount of alcohol from Angostura bitters, which is made with a high-strength spirit. Only a few dashes are used, but the drink is not alcohol-free. Leave out the bitters for a fully alcohol-free version.

### What is the difference between chapman and a mocktail?

Chapman is a specific Nigerian soda-based drink with bitters, grenadine, cucumber and citrus. A mocktail is any alcohol-free mixed drink. Chapman without bitters is technically a mocktail.

### Can Muslims drink chapman?

Many Muslims avoid chapman because the Angostura bitters contains alcohol. A chapman made without bitters, or a fresh-juice mocktail, is a safer choice. If in doubt, ask your guest or your religious leader.

### What can I use instead of Angostura bitters?

Try a squeeze of fresh lime, a little fresh ginger juice, a few mint leaves or a splash of strong cold hibiscus (zobo). They add the sharp, complex edge that bitters gives.

### Is chapman healthy?

Chapman is a treat, not a health drink. Two sodas plus syrup make it high in added sugar. A fresh-juice mocktail topped with sparkling water is a lighter choice, though fruit juice still counts as sugar, so keep glasses small.
