Juice drinks such as 5Alive are cheaper and sweeter, with added sugar. Fresh cold-pressed juice tastes closest to the fruit but must stay cold and be drunk within days. Read the label either way.

## How we compared juice brands

We looked at each brand or type of juice on six things. These are the questions most Nigerian shoppers ask us, and the ones that matter for health and value.

1. **What is in it.** Is it 100% juice, a nectar, or a "juice drink" with water and sugar added? We used the brand's own ingredient lists where published.
2. **Added sugar and sweeteners.** Does the label list sugar, syrup or non-nutritive sweeteners?
3. **How it is made.** Fresh-pressed, or made shelf-stable (for example from concentrate, or packed to keep without a fridge)?
4. **Shelf life and storage.** Can it sit in a cupboard for months, or does it need a fridge and drinking within days?
5. **Where you can buy it.** Supermarkets nationwide, or delivery in one city?
6. **Price per serving.** Roughly, is it an everyday buy or a treat?

We did not do lab tests, blind taste tests or nutrition analysis of other brands' products. Where a brand does not publish something, we say so rather than guess.

A note on health: the [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/news/item/04-03-2015-who-calls-on-countries-to-reduce-sugars-intake-among-adults-and-children) counts the sugars in all fruit juice, fresh or packaged, as "free sugars" to keep low, and the [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/5-a-day/5-a-day-what-counts/) suggests no more than 150ml of juice a day. That applies to every brand below, including ours.

## Juice types in Nigeria, explained

Before the brands, it helps to know the three main types you will see on the shelf.

- **100% juice.** Only fruit (or vegetable) juice. It may be fresh or made from concentrate, where water is removed and later added back.
- **Nectar.** Fruit juice or purée, plus water and usually sugar.
- **Juice drink.** Water, some juice (often from concentrate), sugar or sweeteners, and flavourings. Usually the cheapest and sweetest.

The picture on the front can look the same for all three. The words on the pack, and the ingredients list on the back, tell you which one you have. Our guide to [fresh juice vs packaged juice](/blog/fresh-juice-vs-packaged-juice-nigeria) goes deeper.

## The brands compared

**Chivita (Chivita|Hollandia, Lagos)**

- **What it is:** one of Nigeria's best-known packaged fruit juice brands, sold in cartons. Its range includes orange, pineapple, apple and red grape blends.
- **Sugar:** the brand's website says its fruit juice has no added sugar, no preservatives and no artificial colours or flavours. Some products are labelled as blends, so check the pack for exactly which fruits are inside.
- **How it is made:** packed in aseptic (sealed, sterile) cartons. The website does not say whether the juice is from concentrate.
- **Shelf life:** long-life. Keeps in a cupboard until opened, then refrigerate.
- **Where to buy:** supermarkets and shops nationwide.
- **Good for:** a 100%-style juice you can stock at home, take on a road trip or buy anywhere in Nigeria.

**5Alive (The Coca-Cola Company, bottled by Nigerian Bottling Company)**

- **What it is:** a range of fruit juice drinks, including Berry Blast, Mango, Cocopine and Pulpy Orange, according to [Coca-Cola Nigeria's 5Alive page](https://www.coca-cola.com/ng/en/brands/5alive).
- **Sugar:** the ingredient lists published there include sugar, and some flavours also list non-nutritive sweeteners. Juice content varies by flavour and is often from concentrate; for example, Berry Blast lists concentrated fruit juices at 19.8%.
- **Shelf life:** long-life, sold chilled or at room temperature.
- **Where to buy:** almost everywhere, from supermarkets to roadside kiosks.
- **Good for:** an affordable, widely available sweet drink. It is a juice drink rather than 100% juice, so it is closer to a soft drink in sugar terms.

**Market and roadside fresh juice, zobo and kunu**

- **What it is:** juice, zobo or kunu made by local vendors, often the same day.
- **Sugar:** varies. Some is pure fruit. Zobo and kunu from vendors often have sugar added, sometimes a lot. Ask before you buy.
- **How it is made:** blended, squeezed or boiled by hand. Hygiene and storage vary widely from vendor to vendor.
- **Shelf life:** very short. Drink it the same day and keep it cold.
- **Good for:** cheap, local and fresh when you know and trust the vendor.

**Juice bars and cold-pressed brands in Lagos (including Mrs. Samuel)**

- **What it is:** fresh juice pressed from whole fruit and vegetables, usually in small batches.
- **Sugar:** depends on the brand. Many, including ours, add no sugar. Natural fruit sugar is still there.
- **How it is made:** cold-pressing squeezes juice out with pressure rather than heat. Most of these juices are not pasteurised.
- **Shelf life:** short, usually a few days, refrigerated.
- **Where to buy:** delivery and pick-up, mostly within Lagos.
- **Good for:** juice that tastes closest to the fruit, and vegetable blends you will not find in a carton. It costs more per bottle than cartons.

## So which juice brand is best?

There is no single winner, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. A fair summary:

- **For keeping a stock at home or travelling:** a 100% juice carton such as Chivita makes sense. Check the label for "100%" and no added sugar.
- **For a cheap, sweet treat:** juice drinks like 5Alive are everywhere. Treat them like a soft drink, not a fruit serving.
- **For the freshest taste and vegetable blends:** cold-pressed juice from a trusted Lagos brand. Keep it cold and drink it quickly.
- **For your health overall:** whole fruit and water beat every juice on this page. Juice is an extra.

If you have diabetes or another medical condition, talk to your doctor before adding juice of any brand to your daily routine. Our [juice and diabetes guide](/blog/juice-and-diabetes) explains why.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best juice brand in Nigeria?

It depends on what you need. Long-life 100% juice cartons are convenient and widely available, juice drinks are cheap but contain added sugar, and fresh cold-pressed juice tastes closest to the fruit but must be kept cold and drunk within days.

### Is Chivita 100% juice?

Chivita's website says its fruit juice has no added sugar, no preservatives and no artificial colours or flavours. Some products are described as juice blends. Check the pack for the exact wording and ingredients, as ranges change.

### Does 5Alive contain sugar?

Yes. The ingredient lists on Coca-Cola Nigeria's 5Alive page include sugar, and some flavours also contain non-nutritive sweeteners. 5Alive is a range of juice drinks rather than 100% juice.

### Is fresh juice healthier than packaged juice?

Fresh juice skips concentrating and heat, and many fresh brands add no sugar, so it can keep more of the fruit's flavour. But both contain natural sugar and lack most fibre. Whole fruit is healthier than any juice, fresh or packaged.

### Why is cold-pressed juice more expensive?

It uses a lot of fresh fruit and vegetables per bottle, is made in small batches, and needs cold storage and fast delivery because it has no preservatives. Cartons made at large scale from concentrate cost less to produce and store.
