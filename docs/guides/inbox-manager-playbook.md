# Mrs. Samuel Inbox: Manager's Playbook

For the manager who will set the Inbox up and run it day to day. Last updated 18 November 2026.

It has three parts: what to set up this week, everything the Inbox can do today, and how to get the most out of it. The step-by-step guide for staff, with every button explained, is the separate **Inbox Staff Guide**.

## The short version

The Inbox puts every customer WhatsApp conversation in one shared place, with an owner, a team and a full history. It works today. What makes it pay off is the setup around it: the right teams, opening hours, a library of ready-made answers, a short list of labels, approved templates, and a daily routine. Most of that is an afternoon's work and is listed below.

# Part 2: Everything the Inbox does today

## Talking to customers

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| One shared inbox for the business WhatsApp number | No more customer chats locked in one person's phone |
| Text, photos, videos, voice notes and documents, in and out | Customers send payment proof, delivery photos and voice notes; staff send menus and pictures |
| Sent, delivered and read ticks | Staff know whether the customer has seen the reply |
| Clear "Failed" and "Not confirmed" warnings with the reason | Problems are visible instead of silent |
| Replies always go from the business number | The customer deals with the business, not an individual |
| Live updates with no refreshing, and an unread count in the menu | New messages are seen as they arrive |
| Works on a phone browser as well as a computer | Staff can answer away from the desk |

## Organising the work

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Five lists: Mine, Unassigned, Team, All open, Closed | Everyone knows what is theirs and what is waiting |
| Assign a chat to a person or a team | Clear ownership; nothing sits unowned |
| Automatic assignment to whoever replies first | No time lost deciding who takes it |
| Open, pending and closed statuses | A true picture of outstanding work |
| A closed chat reopens if the customer writes within 7 days | Follow-ups stay in one thread |
| Labels on conversations, with a filter | Find all complaints, bulk enquiries or delivery issues at once |
| Search by name or phone number | Any customer found in seconds |
| Internal notes that only staff see | Hand-overs without the customer seeing the discussion |
| "Someone is typing" between staff | Two people do not answer the same customer |

## Knowing the customer

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| One record per customer, shared with the shop | A customer who orders online and messages on WhatsApp is the same person |
| Name and private notes on each contact | Preferences, allergies and delivery instructions are remembered |
| A link from the chat to the customer's orders | Check an order while answering |
| The customer's other conversations listed beside the chat | Full history at a glance |
| Merge duplicate contacts (owner) | One clean record per person |
| Permanently delete a customer's messages on request (owner) | Meets the legal duty when someone asks |
| Export all contacts to a spreadsheet (owner and admins) | A record of the customer base |

## Answering faster

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Quick replies | Common questions answered in two clicks, in the same words every time |
| Approved templates with blanks to fill | The only way to message a customer after 24 hours of silence: order updates, reminders, follow-ups |
| The 24-hour rule enforced on screen | Staff cannot send something WhatsApp would reject |

## Structure and control

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Teams with team leads | Chats reach the right people |
| More than one business number, each with its own team and hours | Room to add a second line later, for example wholesale |
| Control over which staff may use which number | Sensitive lines stay with the right people |
| Opening hours and holidays | The system knows when the business is closed |
| Separate permissions: view, reply, assign, close, notes, templates, export, settings, reports | Each person gets only what their job needs |
| Managers see their team's chats; owners and admins see all | Privacy between teams, oversight for the owner |
| Audit log in plain sentences | A record of who did what |
| Number status and quality rating from WhatsApp | Early warning before WhatsApp restricts the number |

## Measuring

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Messages in, new conversations, share that got a reply | Demand, and whether it is being met |
| Typical time to first reply and to close | Speed of service |
| Messages per day chart | Busy days, for planning staff |
| By staff member: replies sent | Who is carrying the load |
| By number | Which line is busiest |
| Any date range, with quick 7, 30 and 90-day buttons | Compare weeks and months |

## Built and waiting to be switched on

These exist in the system and start working when the business phone line is connected.

| Function | What it will give the business |
|---|---|
| Answer and make calls in the browser, with mute, hold and transfer | A proper business phone line without desk phones |
| A phone menu ("press 1 for orders") and ringing a whole team | Callers reach the right people |
| Voicemail and call recording | Nothing missed, and a record of what was agreed |
| An automatic WhatsApp message after a missed call | Callers know they will be called back |
| Calls, missed calls and voicemails in the customer's conversation | Phone and WhatsApp history together |
| Call figures in the reports | Missed-call rate and talk time |

## What it does not do yet

Better to know these now than to discover them with a customer.

| Not yet | What to do meanwhile |
|---|---|
| Send templates that have a picture, video or document at the top | Use text-only templates. This is being built. |
| Reply automatically on WhatsApp when the business is closed | Answer first thing in the morning. This is being built. |
| Show the customer's orders inside the chat | Click **Orders & customer profile** on the contact |
| Instagram and Facebook messages | Keep answering those in their own apps for now |
| Send one message to many customers at once | Not available. Do not use personal phones for bulk messages; it risks the business number. |
| Create an order or payment link from the chat | Take the order in the shop system as today |
| Reminders and tasks ("call back Friday") | Use an internal note and the **Pending** status |

# Part 3: How to get the most out of it

## 1. Set the standard for speed

Customers on WhatsApp expect an answer in minutes. Suggested targets:

| Measure | Target during opening hours |
|---|---|
| First reply to a new message | Within 10 minutes |
| Nothing left in **Unassigned** | Longer than 15 minutes |
| A chat closed | The same day, unless waiting on the customer |
| "Got a reply" in the reports | 100% |

Put the targets where the team can see them. Review them every week against the reports.

## 2. Run a daily routine

**Opening (8am, 10 minutes)**
1. Open **Unassigned**. Answer everything that came in overnight, oldest first.
2. Open **Mine**. Deal with anything still open from yesterday.
3. Check **Settings → Numbers**: status **Connected**, quality **Good**.

**During the day**
- One named person watches **Unassigned** at all times. Agree who covers breaks.
- Reply, label, and close as you go. A chat is not finished until it is closed.
- If a chat needs someone else, leave an internal note first, then assign it.

**Closing (7:45pm, 10 minutes)**
1. Empty **Unassigned**.
2. For each open chat in **Mine**: close it, or leave a note saying what happens next.
3. Tell customers who are still waiting when they will hear back.

**Remember the 24 hours.** A customer who wrote at 7pm can be answered freely until 7pm the next day. After that only a template works, and templates cost money. Clearing the Inbox morning and evening keeps almost every reply free.

## 3. Use a short, fixed list of labels

Labels only help if everyone uses the same words. Start with these and add slowly.

| Label | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `order` | The customer is placing or changing an order |
| `delivery` | Where is my order, address changes, rider problems |
| `payment` | Payment proof, failed payments, refunds |
| `complaint` | Anything the customer is unhappy about |
| `bulk` | Events, offices, resellers, large orders |
| `preorder` | Orders for a later date |
| `feedback` | Praise and suggestions |
| `vip` | Regular or high-value customers |

Once a week, filter by `complaint` and read them all. That is the cheapest customer research the business will ever get.

## 4. Build the quick-reply library

Each quick reply has a short name staff see and the message that is inserted. These are drafts. **Check every fact and fill the brackets before saving.**

| Short name | Message |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Hello, and thank you for contacting Mrs. Samuel Fruit Juice. How can we help you today? |
| Opening hours | We are open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm, and Sunday, 10am to 8pm. |
| Address | You can find us at 30 Asa Afariogun Street, opposite Access Bank, Ajao Estate, Lagos. |
| How to order | You can order on our website at mrssamuel.com, or tell us here what you would like and we will help you place it. |
| Menu and prices | Our full menu with current prices is at mrssamuel.com/juices. Is there a flavour you have in mind? |
| Delivery areas | We deliver within Lagos. Please send your delivery address and we will confirm the fee and time for you. |
| Delivery time | Orders for juices in stock go out the same morning or the next morning, depending on when you order. Preorders are delivered on the date you choose. |
| Payment received | Thank you, we have received your payment. Your order is being prepared. |
| Order on the way | Good news: your order is on its way. The rider will call you when close. |
| Storage | Our juice has no preservatives, so please keep it in the fridge and drink it within [number] days. |
| Bulk orders | We would love to help with your event or office order. How many bottles do you need, and for what date? |
| Complaint first reply | I am sorry to hear that. Please send your order number and a photo if you can, and I will sort this out for you right away. |
| Returns | If something is wrong with your order, please tell us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo and your order number. |
| Out of stock | I am sorry, [flavour] is sold out today. It can be preordered for [date], or may I suggest [alternative]? |
| Closing | Thank you for choosing Mrs. Samuel. Enjoy your juice. |

Rules for quick replies:
- Keep each under three sentences.
- One fact per reply, so staff can combine them.
- Review them monthly. Prices, hours and delivery rules change.
- When staff type the same answer three times in a week, it should become a quick reply.

## 5. Get the right templates approved

Templates are written in WhatsApp Manager and approved by WhatsApp. Curly brackets are blanks staff fill in when sending. Suggested first set, all text-only:

| Name | Type | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| `order_confirmed` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, thank you for your order {{2}} with Mrs. Samuel Fruit Juice. We are preparing it now. |
| `order_out_for_delivery` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, your order {{2}} is on its way and should reach you by {{3}}. |
| `payment_reminder` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, your order {{2}} is waiting for payment. Reply here if you need any help completing it. |
| `follow_up` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, this is Mrs. Samuel Fruit Juice following up on your recent message. How can we help? |
| `preorder_ready` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, your preorder {{2}} is ready and will be delivered on {{3}}. |
| `feedback_request` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, we hope you enjoyed your order from Mrs. Samuel. We would love to hear what you thought. |

Rules for templates:
- **Service-update templates are for something the customer is already part of:** their order, their payment, their question.
- **Marketing templates** (offers, new flavours) may only go to customers who have agreed to receive offers. They also cost more. Hold these until there is a proper record of who has agreed.
- WhatsApp rejects templates that are vague, shouty or misleading. Write them the way you would speak to a customer at the counter.
- Every template delivered is charged to the business. Do not send one when a reply inside the 24 hours would do.

## 6. Use notes so the business remembers

After any conversation where you learn something lasting, add it to **Notes about this customer**:

- Delivery details: gate code, landmark, "call on arrival".
- Preferences: favourite flavours, no ginger, less sweet.
- Health notes the customer chose to share: allergies, diabetic.
- Relationship: "orders for her office every Friday", "complained about a late delivery on 12 Nov, refunded".

The next person to serve that customer starts ahead. Write notes as facts, politely, as if the customer could read them.

## 7. Hand over properly

A hand-over has three steps, always in this order:

1. **Internal note:** what the customer wants, what has been done, what is needed next.
2. **Assign** to the colleague or team.
3. **Tell the customer** who will follow up and by when.

Use **Pending** for chats waiting on the customer or on stock, so **All open** shows true outstanding work.

## 8. Protect the number

The business number is an asset. WhatsApp rates it **Good**, **Watch** or **Low** from how customers react.

- Never message people who did not contact the business or agree to hear from it.
- If a customer asks to stop, stop, and note it on their contact.
- Do not send the same template to the same customer repeatedly.
- Check the quality rating every morning. If it drops to **Watch**, stop all templates except order updates and tell the owner.

A number rated **Low** can be limited or blocked by WhatsApp, and customers then cannot reach the business.

## 9. Review every week

Fifteen minutes, every Monday, in **Inbox reports** with the last 7 days selected.

| Look at | Ask |
|---|---|
| **New conversations** | Is demand rising or falling? What caused a spike? |
| **Got a reply** | Is it 100%? If not, which chats were missed and why? |
| **Typical first reply** | Are we inside the target? Which day was slowest? |
| **Messages per day** | Which days are busiest? Is the right number of people on? |
| **By staff member** | Is the load fair? Does anyone need help or training? |
| Chats labelled `complaint` | What went wrong, and what one thing would have prevented it? |
| Chats labelled `bulk` | Did every enquiry get a quote and a follow-up? |

Write down one improvement each week and check it the next.

## 10. Turn conversations into sales

- **Answer fast.** The customer who gets a reply in two minutes orders. The one who waits an hour has bought elsewhere.
- **Always end with a next step:** "Shall I place that order for you?" or "Would you like it delivered tomorrow morning?"
- **Offer the alternative** when something is sold out. Never end on "not available".
- **Follow up bulk enquiries** the next day if they go quiet. Use the `bulk` label to find them.
- **Thank and remember regulars.** Label them `vip` and note what they like.
- **Ask for feedback** after delivery, and pass praise to the team.
