# Mrs. Samuel Inbox: Staff Guide

Last updated 18 November 2026.

## What the Inbox is

The Inbox is one shared place where the whole team reads and answers customers' WhatsApp messages. Every message a customer sends to the business number lands here, and every reply goes out from the business number, not from anyone's personal phone.

What that gives you:

- **Nothing gets lost.** Every chat has an owner, a team and a status, so everyone can see who is handling what.
- **Anyone can pick up where a colleague stopped.** The full history, including private staff notes, stays with the customer.
- **The customer sees one business,** whoever is replying.

Owners and admins see every conversation. Managers see the conversations of their own team. Staff without Inbox access do not see it in the menu.

## Where to find it

Sign in to the admin site and look at the green menu on the left. Under the heading **Customer messages** there are three links.

| Link | What it is for | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Reading and answering customers. This is where you spend your day. | Everyone who talks to customers |
| **Inbox reports** | Numbers and charts: how many messages came in, how fast the team replied, who handled what. | Owner and managers |
| **Inbox settings** | Setting the Inbox up: business numbers, teams, opening hours, ready-made replies. | Owner and admins |

A red number beside **Inbox** in the menu is the count of conversations with unread messages.

If you cannot see **Customer messages**, click **Refresh app** at the top right. If it is still missing, your account has not been given Inbox access. Ask the owner.

# Part 1: Inbox

The Inbox screen has three columns. On a phone you see one at a time.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Left: **the list** | All the conversations you are allowed to see |
| Middle: **the conversation** | The messages with one customer, and the box where you reply |
| Right: **the contact** | Who the customer is, your notes about them, and their other conversations |

## The list (left)

### The five lists

Across the top are five buttons. Each shows a different set of conversations.

| Button | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Mine** | Conversations assigned to you |
| **Unassigned** | Conversations nobody has picked up yet. Check this often. |
| **Team** | Conversations belonging to your team |
| **All open** | Every conversation still being handled |
| **Closed** | Conversations that are finished |

### Finding a conversation

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search name or phone…** | Type part of a name or number to find a customer |
| **All numbers** | If the business has more than one number, show only one of them |
| **All labels** | Show only conversations carrying a label, such as "delivery" or "complaint" |
| **Load older** | At the bottom of the list. Brings up earlier conversations. |

### Reading a row

Each row is one customer. It shows their name or number, the start of the latest message, the time, the business number they wrote to, any labels, and who it is assigned to. A red number means unread messages. **Pending** means the chat is waiting on something, usually the customer.

Click a row to open it.

## The conversation (middle)

### The top bar of a conversation

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **← (back arrow)** | On a phone, returns to the list |
| **Contact** | Opens the customer's details and notes. On a wide screen these already show on the right. |
| **Labels** | Add or remove labels. Type them separated by commas, for example `vip, delivery`, then click **Save labels**. |
| **Call** | Phone the customer. Appears once the business phone line is connected. |
| **First dropdown** (shows "Me", a colleague's name, or "Unassigned") | Who is looking after this chat. Pick a name to hand it to them. |
| **Second dropdown** (shows a team name) | Which team the chat belongs to. Pick another team to move it. |
| **Close chat** | Marks the chat as finished. It moves to the **Closed** list. |
| **Reopen chat** | Shown on a closed chat. Brings it back to open. |

If the customer writes again after a chat is closed, it comes back by itself.

### The messages

- Messages from the customer are on the left. Messages from the business are on the right, in green, with the name of the staff member who sent them.
- **Ticks** on a sent message: one tick means sent, two ticks mean delivered, two blue ticks mean the customer has read it.
- A red **Failed** means the message did not go. The reason is shown underneath.
- An amber **Not confirmed** means WhatsApp did not answer in time. The message may or may not have arrived. Check with the customer before sending it again.
- A yellow box with a dashed edge is an **internal note**. Only staff see it.
- Photos open when clicked. Voice notes play in place. Documents have an **Open document** link.
- **Load earlier messages**, at the top, brings up older history.

### The reply box

At the bottom of the conversation are two buttons that decide what you are writing.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp reply** | What you type is sent to the customer |
| **Internal note** | What you type is saved for staff only. The customer never sees it. |

Always check which one is selected before you press send.

With **WhatsApp reply** selected:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Type a reply…** | Write your message. Press Enter to send. |
| **Paperclip** (Attach a file) | Send a photo or a document. You can add a caption. |
| **Quick replies** | Opens the list of ready-made answers. Click one to drop it into the box, then edit it if needed. |
| **Send a template** | Opens the list of approved templates. See "The 24-hour rule" below. |
| **Reply** | Sends the message |

With **Internal note** selected, type the note and click **Add note**.

The first person to reply to an unassigned chat becomes its owner automatically.

### "Reply window closed"

If you see **Reply window closed** above the box, more than 24 hours have passed since the customer last wrote. WhatsApp will not accept an ordinary message. Click **Send a template** to restart the chat. See "The 24-hour rule".

## The contact (right)

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** and phone number | Who the customer is. Change the name if it is wrong or missing. |
| **Orders & customer profile →** | Opens the customer's page in the shop, with their orders |
| **Notes about this customer** | Anything the team should remember: allergies, delivery gate code, preferences. Only staff see these. |
| **Save changes** | Appears once you have edited the name or notes. Click it to keep them. |
| **Other conversations** | Earlier or parallel conversations with the same customer. Click one to open it. |

### Manage contact (owner only)

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Merge with another contact…** | For when the same person appears twice. Search for the contact to keep, then confirm. Their conversations are joined. Their orders are not touched. |
| **Delete messages and calls…** | Permanently removes this customer's messages, call records and recordings, for when a customer asks for their data to be deleted. You must type their phone number to confirm. This cannot be undone. Their orders are kept. |

## The buttons at the top of the page

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search…** | Searches the whole admin site, not only the Inbox |
| **Refresh app** | Reloads the app to get the latest version. Use it if something looks out of date. |
| **Export contacts** | Downloads every contact as a spreadsheet. Owner and admins only. |

## Phone calls

When the business phone line is connected, a small bar appears at the bottom right of every screen.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take calls** switch | On means customer calls ring your browser. Turn it off when you step away. |
| **Dial** | Opens a keypad to call a number |

When a call comes in, a card shows who is calling with **Answer** and **Decline**. During a call you can mute, put the caller on hold, open a keypad, transfer to another team, and hang up. Calls, missed calls and voicemails appear in the customer's conversation alongside their messages.

# Part 2: Everyday jobs, step by step

## Answer a new customer

1. Click **Inbox**, then **Unassigned**.
2. Click the conversation.
3. Read what they wrote.
4. Make sure **WhatsApp reply** is selected.
5. Type your answer and press Enter.

The chat is now yours and appears under **Mine**.

## Use a ready-made answer

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Quick replies**.
3. Click the answer you want. It appears in the box.
4. Change anything that needs changing, then press Enter.

## Send a photo or document

1. Click the **paperclip**.
2. Choose the file.
3. Add a caption if you want one.
4. Click **Reply**.

## Leave a note for a colleague

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Internal note**.
3. Type the note, for example "Customer wants a call back after 4pm".
4. Click **Add note**.

## Hand a chat to someone else

1. Open the conversation.
2. Leave an internal note saying what has been done and what is needed.
3. Click the first dropdown at the top and pick the colleague's name.

To pass it to another team, use the second dropdown instead.

## Message a customer after more than 24 hours

1. Open the conversation. You will see **Reply window closed**.
2. Click **Send a template**.
3. Pick a template. A preview shows what the customer will receive.
4. If the template has blanks, fill them in.
5. Click **Send template**.

When the customer replies, you can write normally again.

## Label a chat

1. Open the conversation and click **Labels**.
2. Type the labels separated by commas, for example `complaint, refund`.
3. Click **Save labels**.

Use the same words each time so the **All labels** filter works. Agree a short list as a team.

## Finish a chat

1. Make sure the customer has what they need.
2. Click **Close chat**.

## Find an old conversation

1. Click **Closed** (or **All open**).
2. Type the customer's name or number in **Search name or phone…**.

## Fix a wrong or missing name

1. Open the conversation and look at the contact on the right (or click **Contact**).
2. Type the correct name in **Name**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

# Part 3: Inbox reports

This screen shows how the team is doing. Nothing here changes anything; it only reports.

## Choosing the dates

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **7 days / 30 days / 90 days** | Quick buttons for the most recent period |
| **From** and **To** | Pick exact dates |
| **All numbers** | Show one business number only |
| **Export contacts** | Downloads every contact as a spreadsheet |

## The numbers in the green banner

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Messages in** | WhatsApp messages received from customers |
| **Calls in** | Calls received |
| **Missed calls** | Calls nobody answered |

## The four cards

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New conversations** | How many customers started a new chat in these dates |
| **Got a reply** | The share of those new chats that a member of staff answered |
| **Typical first reply** | How long a customer usually waits for the first answer. Lower is better. |
| **Typical time to close** | How long a chat usually takes from first message to closed |

"None yet" means there is nothing to measure in the dates chosen.

## The charts

- **WhatsApp messages per day**: one bar per day. "From customers" are messages received. "From us" are messages the business sent.
- **Incoming calls per day**: answered, missed or voicemail, and out of hours.

Hover over a bar to see the figure for that day.

## The tables

- **By staff member**: WhatsApp replies sent, calls answered and talk time, for each person.
- **By number**: the same activity split by business number.

Use these to see who is carrying the load and which number is busiest.

# Part 4: Inbox settings

For the owner and admins. Across the top are seven sections.

## Numbers

The phone numbers customers message and call.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The name staff see and the phone number |
| **Used for** | WhatsApp only, phone calls only, or both |
| **WhatsApp status** | **Connected** means it is working. Underneath is how many new customers the business may message first each day. |
| **Quality** | WhatsApp's rating of the number: **Good**, **Watch** or **Low**. A low rating means customers are blocking or reporting messages; send fewer templates until it recovers. |
| **Live calls** | Calls happening on this number right now |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ Add number** | Adds a business number |
| **Edit** | Changes a number's details |
| **Check status** | Asks WhatsApp for the latest status and quality |
| **Register on WhatsApp** | A one-time step when a new number is first connected. It asks for a 6-digit PIN. Do not press it for a number that already shows **Connected**. |

When adding or editing a number you fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Name staff see** | A short name such as "Main line" |
| **Phone number** | The full number with country code |
| **Used for** | WhatsApp, calls, or both |
| **WhatsApp Business Account ID** and **Phone number ID** | Two numbers copied from the business's WhatsApp account, entered once |
| **Team that gets new chats** | Which team new conversations on this number go to |
| **Opening hours** | Which schedule applies. "Always open" if none. |
| **After-hours message** | Sent automatically to a customer who writes when the business is closed |

Click **Save number** to keep it.

## Teams

Teams group staff so chats and calls reach the right people, for example "Customer care" or "Deliveries".

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New team** | Creates a team. Give it a name and, if you like, what it handles. |
| **Members** | Tick who belongs to the team. Tick **Team lead** for people who supervise it. Click **Save members**. |
| **Edit** | Renames the team or switches it off |

To appear in the list of people, a member of staff must first be given Inbox access in **Admin users**.

## Who can use which number

A grid of staff against business numbers. Tick the numbers each person may send from and receive on, then save. Owners and admins can use every number; their boxes are ticked and locked.

## Hours

When the business is open. This decides when the after-hours message is sent and how calls are handled out of hours.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New schedule** | Creates a set of opening hours. Name it, for example "Shop hours", and set the open and close time for each day. Mark a day **Closed** if you do not open. |
| **Save hours** | Keeps the schedule |
| **Edit** | Changes a schedule |

Under **Holidays**, add the days you are closed all day, such as Christmas Day.

| Field or button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Date** and **Name** | The day and what it is |
| **Applies to** | Every schedule, or one schedule only |
| **Add holiday** | Saves it |
| **Remove** | Deletes a holiday |

A number with no schedule is treated as always open.

## Phone menu

What a caller hears and can choose ("Press 1 for orders, 2 for deliveries"). Used once the phone line is connected.

| Button or field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New menu** | Creates a menu |
| **Menu name** | A name for staff |
| **Greeting** | The words read out to callers, or a recording you upload |
| **Options** | For each key (1, 2, 3…): a label and what happens. The choices are **Ring a team**, **Read out a message**, or **Take a voicemail**. |
| **Save menu** | Keeps it |

Without a menu, calls ring straight through to staff who are taking calls.

## Quick replies

Ready-made answers staff can drop into a chat, such as prices, delivery times or the shop address.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New quick reply** | Creates one. **Short name** is what staff see in the list, for example "Delivery times". **Message** is the text that is inserted. |
| **Edit** | Changes it |
| **Delete** | Removes it |

Keep them short and friendly. Staff can edit the text before sending.

## Templates

Messages approved in advance by WhatsApp. They are the only way to message a customer who has not written in the last 24 hours.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The template's name |
| **Language** | The language it is written in |
| **Type** | Service update, marketing, or security code |
| **Status** | **Approved** templates can be sent. Others are waiting for WhatsApp or were rejected. |
| **Blanks to fill** | How many details staff type in when sending, such as a name or order number |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync from WhatsApp** | Fetches the latest list and approval status |

Templates are written and submitted for approval in the business's WhatsApp account (WhatsApp Manager), not here. After a new one is approved, click **Sync from WhatsApp** and it appears in the list.

Templates that have a picture, video or document at the top cannot be sent from the Inbox yet. Use text-only templates for now.

# Part 5: Rules to remember

## The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp lets a business reply freely for 24 hours after a customer's last message. Each new message from the customer restarts the 24 hours.

After that, the only thing you can send is an approved template. When the customer answers it, normal replies work again.

So: reply while the window is open, and do not leave a chat hanging overnight if you can finish it today.

## Templates cost money

WhatsApp charges the business for each template delivered. Use them when there is a reason, not as a habit.

## Only message people who asked

Never send promotions to customers who have not agreed to receive them. If a customer says stop, stop, and add a note on their contact. Customers who block or report the business lower the number's quality rating.

## Keep it professional

- Customers see the business, not you personally. Write as the business.
- Internal notes are for facts the next colleague needs. Write them as if the customer might one day read them.
- Never ask for, or type, card numbers, PINs or passwords in a chat.

## Privacy

- Notes, messages and recordings are for serving the customer. Do not share them outside the team.
- If a customer asks for their data to be deleted, tell the owner. Only the owner can do it.
- Do not export contacts unless the owner has asked you to.

# Part 6: If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A new message has not appeared | Wait a few seconds; the screen updates by itself. If not, click **Refresh app**. |
| **Reply window closed** | More than 24 hours have passed. Click **Send a template**. |
| A red **Failed** under a message | Read the reason shown. Fix it and send again. If it mentions the reply window, send a template. |
| An amber **Not confirmed** | Do not resend straight away. Check whether the customer received it, or wait a minute for the ticks. |
| A template fails with a message about a picture or header | That template needs a picture, which the Inbox cannot attach yet. Choose a text-only template. |
| You cannot find a conversation | Look under **All open** and **Closed**, and clear the **All numbers** and **All labels** filters. |
| You cannot see a conversation a colleague mentioned | It may belong to another team. Ask them to move it to your team or assign it to you. |
| **Customer messages** is missing from the menu | Click **Refresh app**. If still missing, ask the owner for Inbox access. |
| A number shows a status other than **Connected** | Tell the owner. Customers may not be able to reach the business on WhatsApp. |
| The same customer appears twice | Tell the owner, who can merge the two contacts. |
| Nobody on the team can send anything | Tell the owner straight away. The connection to WhatsApp may need renewing. |
