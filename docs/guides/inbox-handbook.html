What the Inbox is↑ Contents

The Inbox is one shared place where the whole team reads and answers customers' WhatsApp messages. Every message a customer sends to the business number lands here, and every reply goes out from the business number, not from anyone's personal phone.

What that gives you:

  • Nothing gets lost. Every chat has an owner, a team and a status, so everyone can see who is handling what.
  • Anyone can pick up where a colleague stopped. The full history, including private staff notes, stays with the customer.
  • The customer sees one business, whoever is replying.

Owners and admins see every conversation. Managers see the conversations of their own team. Staff without Inbox access do not see it in the menu.

Where to find it

Sign in to the admin site and look at the green menu on the left. Under the heading Customer messages there are three links.

LinkWhat it is forWho uses it
InboxReading and answering customers. This is where you spend your day.Everyone who talks to customers
Inbox reportsNumbers and charts: how many messages came in, how fast the team replied, who handled what.Owner and managers
Inbox settingsSetting the Inbox up: business numbers, teams, opening hours, ready-made replies.Owner and admins

A red number beside Inbox in the menu is the count of conversations with unread messages.

If you cannot see Customer messages, click Refresh app at the top right. If it is still missing, your account has not been given Inbox access. Ask the owner.

Reading a row

Each row is one customer. It shows their name or number, the start of the latest message, the time, the business number they wrote to, any labels, and who it is assigned to. A red number means unread messages. Pending means the chat is waiting on something, usually the customer.

Click a row to open it.

The conversation (middle)

The top bar of a conversation

ButtonWhat it does
← (back arrow)On a phone, returns to the list
ContactOpens the customer's details and notes. On a wide screen these already show on the right.
LabelsAdd or remove labels. Type them separated by commas, for example vip, delivery, then click Save labels.
CallPhone the customer. Appears once the business phone line is connected.
First dropdown (shows "Me", a colleague's name, or "Unassigned")Who is looking after this chat. Pick a name to hand it to them.
Second dropdown (shows a team name)Which team the chat belongs to. Pick another team to move it.
Close chatMarks the chat as finished. It moves to the Closed list.
Reopen chatShown on a closed chat. Brings it back to open.

If the customer writes again within 7 days of a chat being closed, the same chat reopens by itself. After 7 days, their message starts a new chat, and the old one is listed under Other conversations.

The messages

  • Messages from the customer are on the left. Messages from the business are on the right, in green, with the name of the staff member who sent them.
  • Ticks on a sent message: one tick means sent, two ticks mean delivered, two blue ticks mean the customer has read it.
  • A red Failed means the message did not go. The reason is shown underneath.
  • An amber Not confirmed means WhatsApp did not answer in time. The message may or may not have arrived. Check with the customer before sending it again.
  • A yellow box with a dashed edge is an internal note. Only staff see it.
  • Photos open when clicked. Voice notes play in place. Documents have an Open document link.
  • Load earlier messages, at the top, brings up older history.

"Reply window closed"

If you see Reply window closed above the box, more than 24 hours have passed since the customer last wrote. WhatsApp will not accept an ordinary message. Click Send a template to restart the chat. See "The 24-hour rule".

Everyday jobs, step by step↑ Contents

Answer a new customer

  1. Click Inbox, then Unassigned.
  2. Click the conversation.
  3. Read what they wrote.
  4. Make sure WhatsApp reply is selected.
  5. Type your answer and press Enter, or tap Reply on a phone.

The chat is now yours and appears under Mine.

Use a ready-made answer

  1. Open the conversation.
  2. Click Quick replies.
  3. Click the answer you want. It appears in the box.
  4. Change anything that needs changing, then send it.

Send a photo or document

  1. Click the paperclip.
  2. Choose the file.
  3. Add a caption if you want one.
  4. Click Reply.

Leave a note for a colleague

  1. Open the conversation.
  2. Click Internal note.
  3. Type the note, for example "Customer wants a call back after 4pm".
  4. Click Add note.

Hand a chat to someone else

  1. Open the conversation.
  2. Leave an internal note saying what has been done and what is needed.
  3. Click the first dropdown at the top and pick the colleague's name.

To pass it to another team, use the second dropdown instead.

Message a customer after more than 24 hours

  1. Open the conversation. You will see Reply window closed.
  2. Click Send a template.
  3. Pick a template. A preview shows what the customer will receive.
  4. If the template has blanks, fill them in.
  5. Click Send template.

When the customer replies, you can write normally again.

Label a chat

  1. Open the conversation and click Labels.
  2. Type the labels separated by commas, for example complaint, refund.
  3. Click Save labels.

Use the same words each time so the All labels filter works. Agree a short list as a team.

Finish a chat

  1. Make sure the customer has what they need.
  2. Click Close chat.

Find an old conversation

  1. Click Closed (or All open).
  2. Type the customer's name or number in Search name or phone….

Fix a wrong or missing name

  1. Open the conversation and look at the contact on the right (or click Contact).
  2. Type the correct name in Name.
  3. Click Save changes.

Ten practices that make it pay↑ Contents

1. Set the standard for speed

Customers on WhatsApp expect an answer in minutes. Suggested targets:

MeasureTarget during opening hours
First reply to a new messageWithin 10 minutes
Nothing left in UnassignedLonger than 15 minutes
A chat closedThe same day, unless waiting on the customer
"Got a reply" in the reports100%

Put the targets where the team can see them. Review them every week against the reports.

2. Run a daily routine

Opening (8am, 10 minutes)

  1. Open Unassigned. Answer everything that came in overnight, oldest first.
  2. Open Mine. Deal with anything still open from yesterday.
  3. Check Settings → Numbers: status Connected, quality Good.

During the day

  • One named person watches Unassigned at all times. Agree who covers breaks.
  • Reply, label, and close as you go. A chat is not finished until it is closed.
  • If a chat needs someone else, leave an internal note first, then assign it.

Closing (7:45pm, 10 minutes)

  1. Empty Unassigned.
  2. For each open chat in Mine: close it, or leave a note saying what happens next.
  3. Tell customers who are still waiting when they will hear back.

Remember the 24 hours. A customer who wrote at 7pm can be answered freely until 7pm the next day. After that only a template works, and templates cost money. Clearing the Inbox morning and evening keeps almost every reply free.

3. Use a short, fixed list of labels

Labels only help if everyone uses the same words. Start with these and add slowly.

  • One fact per reply, so staff can combine them.

Rules for templates:

  • Service-update templates are for something the customer is already part of: their order, their payment, their question.
  • Marketing templates (offers, new flavours) may only go to customers who have agreed to receive offers. They also cost more. Hold these until there is a proper record of who has agreed.
  • WhatsApp rejects templates that are vague, shouty or misleading. Write them the way you would speak to a customer at the counter.
  • Every template delivered is charged to the business. Do not send one when a reply inside the 24 hours would do.

6. Use notes so the business remembers

After any conversation where you learn something lasting, add it to Notes about this customer:

  • Delivery details: gate code, landmark, "call on arrival".
  • Preferences: favourite flavours, no ginger, less sweet.
  • Health notes the customer chose to share: allergies, diabetic.
  • Relationship: "orders for her office every Friday", "complained about a late delivery on 12 Nov, refunded".

The next person to serve that customer starts ahead. Write notes as facts, politely, as if the customer could read them.

7. Hand over properly

A hand-over has three steps, always in this order:

  1. Internal note: what the customer wants, what has been done, what is needed next.
  2. Assign to the colleague or team.
  3. Tell the customer who will follow up and by when.

Use Pending for chats waiting on the customer or on stock, so All open shows true outstanding work.

8. Protect the number

The business number is an asset. WhatsApp rates it Good, Watch or Low from how customers react.

  • Never message people who did not contact the business or agree to hear from it.
  • If a customer asks to stop, stop, and note it on their contact.
  • Do not send the same template to the same customer repeatedly.
  • Check the quality rating every morning. If it drops to Watch, stop all templates except order updates and tell the owner.

A number rated Low can be limited or blocked by WhatsApp, and customers then cannot reach the business.

10. Turn conversations into sales

  • Answer fast. The customer who gets a reply in two minutes orders. The one who waits an hour has bought elsewhere.
  • Always end with a next step: "Shall I place that order for you?" or "Would you like it delivered tomorrow morning?"
  • Offer the alternative when something is sold out. Never end on "not available".
  • Follow up bulk enquiries the next day if they go quiet. Use the bulk label to find them.
  • Thank and remember regulars. Label them vip and note what they like.
  • Ask for feedback after delivery, and pass praise to the team.

Rules to remember↑ Contents

The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp lets a business reply freely for 24 hours after a customer's last message. Each new message from the customer restarts the 24 hours.

After that, the only thing you can send is an approved template. When the customer answers it, normal replies work again.

So: reply while the window is open, and do not leave a chat hanging overnight if you can finish it today.

Templates cost money

WhatsApp charges the business for each template delivered. Use them when there is a reason, not as a habit.

Only message people who asked

Never send promotions to customers who have not agreed to receive them. If a customer says stop, stop, and add a note on their contact. Customers who block or report the business lower the number's quality rating.

Keep it professional

  • Customers see the business, not you personally. Write as the business.
  • Internal notes are for facts the next colleague needs. Write them as if the customer might one day read them.
  • Never ask for, or type, card numbers, PINs or passwords in a chat.

Privacy

  • Notes, messages and recordings are for serving the customer. Do not share them outside the team.
  • If a customer asks for their data to be deleted, tell the owner. Only the owner can do it.
  • Do not export contacts unless the owner has asked you to.
