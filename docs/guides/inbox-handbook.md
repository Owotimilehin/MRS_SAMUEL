## What the Inbox is

The Inbox is one shared place where the whole team reads and answers customers' WhatsApp messages. Every message a customer sends to the business number lands here, and every reply goes out from the business number, not from anyone's personal phone.

What that gives you:

- **Nothing gets lost.** Every chat has an owner, a team and a status, so everyone can see who is handling what.
- **Anyone can pick up where a colleague stopped.** The full history, including private staff notes, stays with the customer.
- **The customer sees one business,** whoever is replying.

Owners and admins see every conversation. Managers see the conversations of their own team. Staff without Inbox access do not see it in the menu.

### Where to find it

Sign in to the admin site and look at the green menu on the left. Under the heading **Customer messages** there are three links.

| Link | What it is for | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Reading and answering customers. This is where you spend your day. | Everyone who talks to customers |
| **Inbox reports** | Numbers and charts: how many messages came in, how fast the team replied, who handled what. | Owner and managers |
| **Inbox settings** | Setting the Inbox up: business numbers, teams, opening hours, ready-made replies. | Owner and admins |

A red number beside **Inbox** in the menu is the count of conversations with unread messages.

If you cannot see **Customer messages**, click **Refresh app** at the top right. If it is still missing, your account has not been given Inbox access. Ask the owner.

## Everything it does today

### Talking to customers

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| One shared inbox for the business WhatsApp number | No more customer chats locked in one person's phone |
| Text, photos, videos, voice notes and documents, in and out | Customers send payment proof, delivery photos and voice notes; staff send menus and pictures |
| Sent, delivered and read ticks | Staff know whether the customer has seen the reply |
| Clear "Failed" and "Not confirmed" warnings with the reason | Problems are visible instead of silent |
| Replies always go from the business number | The customer deals with the business, not an individual |
| Live updates with no refreshing, and an unread count in the menu | New messages are seen as they arrive |
| Works on a phone browser as well as a computer | Staff can answer away from the desk |

### Organising the work

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Five lists: Mine, Unassigned, Team, All open, Closed | Everyone knows what is theirs and what is waiting |
| Assign a chat to a person or a team | Clear ownership; nothing sits unowned |
| Automatic assignment to whoever replies first | No time lost deciding who takes it |
| Open, pending and closed statuses | A true picture of outstanding work |
| A closed chat reopens if the customer writes within 7 days | Follow-ups stay in one thread |
| Labels on conversations, with a filter | Find all complaints, bulk enquiries or delivery issues at once |
| Search by name or phone number | Any customer found in seconds |
| Internal notes that only staff see | Hand-overs without the customer seeing the discussion |
| "Someone is typing" between staff | Two people do not answer the same customer |

### Knowing the customer

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| One record per customer, shared with the shop | A customer who orders online and messages on WhatsApp is the same person |
| Name and private notes on each contact | Preferences, allergies and delivery instructions are remembered |
| A link from the chat to the customer's orders | Check an order while answering |
| The customer's other conversations listed beside the chat | Full history at a glance |
| Merge duplicate contacts (owner) | One clean record per person |
| Permanently delete a customer's messages on request (owner) | Meets the legal duty when someone asks |
| Export all contacts to a spreadsheet (owner and admins) | A record of the customer base |

### Answering faster

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Quick replies | Common questions answered in two clicks, in the same words every time |
| Approved templates with blanks to fill | The only way to message a customer after 24 hours of silence: order updates, reminders, follow-ups |
| The 24-hour rule enforced on screen | Staff cannot send something WhatsApp would reject |

### Structure and control

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Teams with team leads | Chats reach the right people |
| More than one business number, each with its own team and hours | Room to add a second line later, for example wholesale |
| Control over which staff may use which number | Sensitive lines stay with the right people |
| Opening hours and holidays | The system knows when the business is closed |
| Separate permissions: view, reply, assign, close, notes, templates, export, settings, reports | Each person gets only what their job needs |
| Managers see their team's chats; owners and admins see all | Privacy between teams, oversight for the owner |
| Audit log in plain sentences | A record of who did what |
| Number status and quality rating from WhatsApp | Early warning before WhatsApp restricts the number |

### Measuring

| Function | What it gives the business |
|---|---|
| Messages in, new conversations, share that got a reply | Demand, and whether it is being met |
| Typical time to first reply and to close | Speed of service |
| Messages per day chart | Busy days, for planning staff |
| By staff member: replies sent | Who is carrying the load |
| By number | Which line is busiest |
| Any date range, with quick 7, 30 and 90-day buttons | Compare weeks and months |

### Built and waiting to be switched on

These exist in the system and start working when the business phone line is connected.

| Function | What it will give the business |
|---|---|
| Answer and make calls in the browser, with mute, hold and transfer | A proper business phone line without desk phones |
| A phone menu ("press 1 for orders") and ringing a whole team | Callers reach the right people |
| Voicemail and call recording | Nothing missed, and a record of what was agreed |
| An automatic WhatsApp message after a missed call | Callers know they will be called back |
| Calls, missed calls and voicemails in the customer's conversation | Phone and WhatsApp history together |
| Call figures in the reports | Missed-call rate and talk time |

### What it does not do yet

Better to know these now than to discover them with a customer.

| Not yet | What to do meanwhile |
|---|---|
| Send templates that have a picture, video or document at the top | Use text-only templates. This is being built. |
| Reply automatically on WhatsApp when the business is closed | Answer first thing in the morning. This is being built. |
| Show the customer's orders inside the chat | Click **Orders & customer profile** on the contact |
| Instagram and Facebook messages | Keep answering those in their own apps for now |
| Send one message to many customers at once | Not available. Do not use personal phones for bulk messages; it risks the business number. |
| Create an order or payment link from the chat | Take the order in the shop system as today |
| Reminders and tasks ("call back Friday") | Use an internal note and the **Pending** status |

<a id="the-inbox-screen"></a>

## The Inbox screen

The Inbox screen has three columns. On a phone you see one at a time.

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Left: **the list** | All the conversations you are allowed to see |
| Middle: **the conversation** | The messages with one customer, and the box where you reply |
| Right: **the contact** | Who the customer is, your notes about them, and their other conversations |

### The list (left)

#### The five lists

Across the top are five buttons. Each shows a different set of conversations.

| Button | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Mine** | Conversations assigned to you |
| **Unassigned** | Conversations nobody has picked up yet. Check this often. |
| **Team** | Conversations belonging to your team |
| **All open** | Every conversation still being handled |
| **Closed** | Conversations that are finished |

#### Finding a conversation

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search name or phone…** | Type part of a name or number to find a customer |
| **All numbers** | If the business has more than one number, show only one of them |
| **All labels** | Show only conversations carrying a label, such as "delivery" or "complaint" |
| **Load older** | At the bottom of the list. Brings up earlier conversations. |

#### Reading a row

Each row is one customer. It shows their name or number, the start of the latest message, the time, the business number they wrote to, any labels, and who it is assigned to. A red number means unread messages. **Pending** means the chat is waiting on something, usually the customer.

Click a row to open it.

### The conversation (middle)

#### The top bar of a conversation

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **← (back arrow)** | On a phone, returns to the list |
| **Contact** | Opens the customer's details and notes. On a wide screen these already show on the right. |
| **Labels** | Add or remove labels. Type them separated by commas, for example `vip, delivery`, then click **Save labels**. |
| **Call** | Phone the customer. Appears once the business phone line is connected. |
| **First dropdown** (shows "Me", a colleague's name, or "Unassigned") | Who is looking after this chat. Pick a name to hand it to them. |
| **Second dropdown** (shows a team name) | Which team the chat belongs to. Pick another team to move it. |
| **Close chat** | Marks the chat as finished. It moves to the **Closed** list. |
| **Reopen chat** | Shown on a closed chat. Brings it back to open. |

If the customer writes again within 7 days of a chat being closed, the same chat reopens by itself. After 7 days, their message starts a new chat, and the old one is listed under **Other conversations**.

#### The messages

- Messages from the customer are on the left. Messages from the business are on the right, in green, with the name of the staff member who sent them.
- **Ticks** on a sent message: one tick means sent, two ticks mean delivered, two blue ticks mean the customer has read it.
- A red **Failed** means the message did not go. The reason is shown underneath.
- An amber **Not confirmed** means WhatsApp did not answer in time. The message may or may not have arrived. Check with the customer before sending it again.
- A yellow box with a dashed edge is an **internal note**. Only staff see it.
- Photos open when clicked. Voice notes play in place. Documents have an **Open document** link.
- **Load earlier messages**, at the top, brings up older history.

#### The reply box

At the bottom of the conversation are two buttons that decide what you are writing.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **WhatsApp reply** | What you type is sent to the customer |
| **Internal note** | What you type is saved for staff only. The customer never sees it. |

Always check which one is selected before you press send.

With **WhatsApp reply** selected:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Type a reply…** | Write your message. On a computer, press Enter to send (Shift+Enter starts a new line). On a phone, tap **Reply**. |
| **Paperclip** (Attach a file) | Send a photo or a document. You can add a caption. |
| **Quick replies** | Opens the list of ready-made answers. Click one to drop it into the box, then edit it if needed. |
| **Send a template** | Opens the list of approved templates. See "The 24-hour rule" below. |
| **Reply** | Sends the message |

With **Internal note** selected, type the note and click **Add note**.

The first person to reply to an unassigned chat becomes its owner automatically.

#### "Reply window closed"

If you see **Reply window closed** above the box, more than 24 hours have passed since the customer last wrote. WhatsApp will not accept an ordinary message. Click **Send a template** to restart the chat. See "The 24-hour rule".

### The contact (right)

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** and phone number | Who the customer is. Change the name if it is wrong or missing. |
| **Orders & customer profile →** | Opens the customer's page in the shop, with their orders |
| **Notes about this customer** | Anything the team should remember: allergies, delivery gate code, preferences. Only staff see these. |
| **Save changes** | Appears once you have edited the name or notes. Click it to keep them. |
| **Other conversations** | Earlier or parallel conversations with the same customer. Click one to open it. |

#### Manage contact (owner only)

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Merge with another contact…** | For when the same person appears twice. Search for the contact to keep, then confirm. Their conversations are joined. Their orders are not touched. |
| **Delete messages and calls…** | Permanently removes this customer's messages, call records and recordings, for when a customer asks for their data to be deleted. You must type their phone number to confirm. This cannot be undone. Their orders are kept. |

### The buttons at the top of the page

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search…** | Searches the whole admin site, not only the Inbox |
| **Refresh app** | Reloads the app to get the latest version. Use it if something looks out of date. |
| **Export contacts** | Downloads every contact as a spreadsheet. Owner and admins only. |

### Phone calls

When the business phone line is connected, a small bar appears at the bottom right of every screen.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take calls** switch | On means customer calls ring your browser. Turn it off when you step away. |
| **Dial** | Opens a keypad to call a number |

When a call comes in, a card shows who is calling with **Answer** and **Decline**. During a call you can mute, put the caller on hold, open a keypad, transfer to another team, and hang up. Calls, missed calls and voicemails appear in the customer's conversation alongside their messages.

<a id="inbox-reports"></a>

## Inbox reports

This screen shows how the team is doing. Nothing here changes anything; it only reports.

### Choosing the dates

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **7 days / 30 days / 90 days** | Quick buttons for the most recent period |
| **From** and **To** | Pick exact dates |
| **All numbers** | Show one business number only |
| **Export contacts** | Downloads every contact as a spreadsheet |

### The numbers in the green banner

| Number | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Messages in** | WhatsApp messages received from customers |
| **Calls in** | Calls received |
| **Missed calls** | Calls nobody answered |

### The four cards

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New conversations** | How many customers started a new chat in these dates |
| **Got a reply** | The share of those new chats that a member of staff answered |
| **Typical first reply** | How long a customer usually waits for the first answer. Lower is better. |
| **Typical time to close** | How long a chat usually takes from first message to closed |

"None yet" means there is nothing to measure in the dates chosen.

### The charts

- **WhatsApp messages per day**: one bar per day. "From customers" are messages received. "From us" are messages the business sent.
- **Incoming calls per day**: answered, missed or voicemail, and out of hours.

Hover over a bar to see the figure for that day.

### The tables

- **By staff member**: WhatsApp replies sent, calls answered and talk time, for each person.
- **By number**: the same activity split by business number.

Use these to see who is carrying the load and which number is busiest.

<a id="inbox-settings"></a>

## Inbox settings

For the owner and admins. Across the top are seven sections.

### Numbers

The phone numbers customers message and call.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Number** | The name staff see and the phone number |
| **Used for** | WhatsApp only, phone calls only, or both |
| **WhatsApp status** | **Connected** means it is working. Underneath is how many new customers the business may message first each day. |
| **Quality** | WhatsApp's rating of the number: **Good**, **Watch** or **Low**. A low rating means customers are blocking or reporting messages; send fewer templates until it recovers. |
| **Live calls** | Calls happening on this number right now |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ Add number** | Adds a business number |
| **Edit** | Changes a number's details |
| **Check status** | Asks WhatsApp for the latest status and quality |
| **Register on WhatsApp** | A one-time step when a new number is first connected. It asks for a 6-digit PIN. Do not press it for a number that already shows **Connected**. |

When adding or editing a number you fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Name staff see** | A short name such as "Main line" |
| **Phone number** | The full number with country code |
| **Used for** | WhatsApp, calls, or both |
| **WhatsApp Business Account ID** and **Phone number ID** | Two numbers copied from the business's WhatsApp account, entered once |
| **Team that gets new chats** | Which team new conversations on this number go to |
| **Opening hours** | Which schedule applies. "Always open" if none. |
| **After-hours message** | Read out to customers who phone when the business is closed. WhatsApp messages that arrive out of hours are not answered automatically; they wait in the Inbox for the team. |

Click **Save number** to keep it.

### Teams

Teams group staff so chats and calls reach the right people, for example "Customer care" or "Deliveries".

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New team** | Creates a team. Give it a name and, if you like, what it handles. |
| **Members** | Tick who belongs to the team. Tick **Team lead** for people who supervise it. Click **Save members**. |
| **Edit** | Renames the team or switches it off |

To appear in the list of people, a member of staff must first be given Inbox access in **Admin users**.

### Who can use which number

A grid of staff against business numbers. Tick the numbers each person may send from and receive on, then save. Owners and admins can use every number; their boxes are ticked and locked.

### Hours

When the business is open. This decides how phone calls are handled out of hours and what counts as "after hours" in the reports.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New schedule** | Creates a set of opening hours. Name it, for example "Shop hours", and set the open and close time for each day. Mark a day **Closed** if you do not open. |
| **Save hours** | Keeps the schedule |
| **Edit** | Changes a schedule |

Under **Holidays**, add the days you are closed all day, such as Christmas Day.

| Field or button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Date** and **Name** | The day and what it is |
| **Applies to** | Every schedule, or one schedule only |
| **Add holiday** | Saves it |
| **Remove** | Deletes a holiday |

A number with no schedule is treated as always open.

### Phone menu

What a caller hears and can choose ("Press 1 for orders, 2 for deliveries"). Used once the phone line is connected.

| Button or field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New menu** | Creates a menu |
| **Menu name** | A name for staff |
| **Greeting** | The words read out to callers, or a recording you upload |
| **Options** | For each key (1, 2, 3…): a label and what happens. The choices are **Ring a team**, **Read out a message**, or **Take a voicemail**. |
| **Save menu** | Keeps it |

Without a menu, calls ring straight through to staff who are taking calls.

### Quick replies

Ready-made answers staff can drop into a chat, such as prices, delivery times or the shop address.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **+ New quick reply** | Creates one. **Short name** is what staff see in the list, for example "Delivery times". **Message** is the text that is inserted. |
| **Edit** | Changes it |
| **Delete** | Removes it |

Keep them short and friendly. Staff can edit the text before sending.

### Templates

Messages approved in advance by WhatsApp. They are the only way to message a customer who has not written in the last 24 hours.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The template's name |
| **Language** | The language it is written in |
| **Type** | Service update, Marketing, or Login code |
| **Status** | **Approved** templates can be sent. Others are waiting for WhatsApp or were rejected. |
| **Blanks to fill** | How many details staff type in when sending, such as a name or order number |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync from WhatsApp** | Fetches the latest list and approval status |

Templates are written and submitted for approval in the business's WhatsApp account (WhatsApp Manager), not here. After a new one is approved, click **Sync from WhatsApp** and it appears in the list.

Templates that have a picture, video or document at the top cannot be sent from the Inbox yet. Use text-only templates for now.

<a id="a-before-the-order-turning-questions-into-sales"></a>

## A. Before the order: turning questions into sales

| Use case | How it works in the Inbox | Set up needed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Menu and price enquiry** | Customer asks what you sell. Staff send the "Menu and prices" quick reply and ask what they have in mind. | Quick reply | Now |
| **Flavour recommendation** | Customer says "something not too sweet" or "for my kids". Staff suggest two flavours and note the preference on the contact. | Staff know the range; notes habit | Now |
| **Ingredient and health questions** | "Is there added sugar?", "What is in Pure Green?" Staff answer from the product list. Health advice is never given; ingredients only. | Quick replies for the common ones | Now |
| **Is it in stock today?** | Staff check the branch stock screen and answer. | Access to branch stock | Now |
| **First-time customer** | Greet, ask what they are looking for, explain delivery and how to order, save their name on the contact. | "Greeting" and "How to order" quick replies | Now |
| **Order taken in the chat** | Customer lists what they want. Staff confirm items, size, address and time, then enter the sale at the till choosing WhatsApp as the source. | Till access for the person answering, or a hand-over note to the branch | Now |
| **Preorder for a later date** | For 330ml bottles, specials or large quantities. Staff agree the date, enter the preorder, and label the chat `preorder`. | `preorder` label | Now |
| **Order for pickup** | Customer wants to collect. Staff confirm the time, and message when it is ready. | "Ready for pickup" quick reply | Now |

<a id="everyday-jobs-step-by-step"></a>

## Everyday jobs, step by step

### Answer a new customer

1. Click **Inbox**, then **Unassigned**.
2. Click the conversation.
3. Read what they wrote.
4. Make sure **WhatsApp reply** is selected.
5. Type your answer and press Enter, or tap **Reply** on a phone.

The chat is now yours and appears under **Mine**.

### Use a ready-made answer

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Quick replies**.
3. Click the answer you want. It appears in the box.
4. Change anything that needs changing, then send it.

### Send a photo or document

1. Click the **paperclip**.
2. Choose the file.
3. Add a caption if you want one.
4. Click **Reply**.

### Leave a note for a colleague

1. Open the conversation.
2. Click **Internal note**.
3. Type the note, for example "Customer wants a call back after 4pm".
4. Click **Add note**.

### Hand a chat to someone else

1. Open the conversation.
2. Leave an internal note saying what has been done and what is needed.
3. Click the first dropdown at the top and pick the colleague's name.

To pass it to another team, use the second dropdown instead.

### Message a customer after more than 24 hours

1. Open the conversation. You will see **Reply window closed**.
2. Click **Send a template**.
3. Pick a template. A preview shows what the customer will receive.
4. If the template has blanks, fill them in.
5. Click **Send template**.

When the customer replies, you can write normally again.

### Label a chat

1. Open the conversation and click **Labels**.
2. Type the labels separated by commas, for example `complaint, refund`.
3. Click **Save labels**.

Use the same words each time so the **All labels** filter works. Agree a short list as a team.

### Finish a chat

1. Make sure the customer has what they need.
2. Click **Close chat**.

### Find an old conversation

1. Click **Closed** (or **All open**).
2. Type the customer's name or number in **Search name or phone…**.

### Fix a wrong or missing name

1. Open the conversation and look at the contact on the right (or click **Contact**).
2. Type the correct name in **Name**.
3. Click **Save changes**.

<a id="ten-practices-that-make-it-pay"></a>

## Ten practices that make it pay

### 1. Set the standard for speed

Customers on WhatsApp expect an answer in minutes. Suggested targets:

| Measure | Target during opening hours |
|---|---|
| First reply to a new message | Within 10 minutes |
| Nothing left in **Unassigned** | Longer than 15 minutes |
| A chat closed | The same day, unless waiting on the customer |
| "Got a reply" in the reports | 100% |

Put the targets where the team can see them. Review them every week against the reports.

### 2. Run a daily routine

**Opening (8am, 10 minutes)**
1. Open **Unassigned**. Answer everything that came in overnight, oldest first.
2. Open **Mine**. Deal with anything still open from yesterday.
3. Check **Settings → Numbers**: status **Connected**, quality **Good**.

**During the day**
- One named person watches **Unassigned** at all times. Agree who covers breaks.
- Reply, label, and close as you go. A chat is not finished until it is closed.
- If a chat needs someone else, leave an internal note first, then assign it.

**Closing (7:45pm, 10 minutes)**
1. Empty **Unassigned**.
2. For each open chat in **Mine**: close it, or leave a note saying what happens next.
3. Tell customers who are still waiting when they will hear back.

**Remember the 24 hours.** A customer who wrote at 7pm can be answered freely until 7pm the next day. After that only a template works, and templates cost money. Clearing the Inbox morning and evening keeps almost every reply free.

### 3. Use a short, fixed list of labels

Labels only help if everyone uses the same words. Start with these and add slowly.

| Label | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `order` | The customer is placing or changing an order |
| `delivery` | Where is my order, address changes, rider problems |
| `payment` | Payment proof, failed payments, refunds |
| `complaint` | Anything the customer is unhappy about |
| `bulk` | Events, offices, resellers, large orders |
| `preorder` | Orders for a later date |
| `feedback` | Praise and suggestions |
| `vip` | Regular or high-value customers |

Once a week, filter by `complaint` and read them all. That is the cheapest customer research the business will ever get.

### 4. Build the quick-reply library

Each quick reply has a short name staff see and the message that is inserted. These are drafts. **Check every fact and fill the brackets before saving.**

| Short name | Message |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Hello, and thank you for contacting Mrs. Samuel Fruit Juice. How can we help you today? |
| Opening hours | We are open Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm, and Sunday, 10am to 8pm. |
| Address | You can find us at 30 Asa Afariogun Street, opposite Access Bank, Ajao Estate, Lagos. |
| How to order | You can order on our website at mrssamuel.com, or tell us here what you would like and we will help you place it. |
| Menu and prices | Our full menu with current prices is at mrssamuel.com/juices. Is there a flavour you have in mind? |
| Delivery areas | We deliver within Lagos. Please send your delivery address and we will confirm the fee and time for you. |
| Delivery time | Orders for juices in stock go out the same morning or the next morning, depending on when you order. Preorders are delivered on the date you choose. |
| Payment received | Thank you, we have received your payment. Your order is being prepared. |
| Order on the way | Good news: your order is on its way. The rider will call you when close. |
| Storage | Our juice has no preservatives, so please keep it in the fridge and drink it within [number] days. |
| Bulk orders | We would love to help with your event or office order. How many bottles do you need, and for what date? |
| Complaint first reply | I am sorry to hear that. Please send your order number and a photo if you can, and I will sort this out for you right away. |
| Returns | If something is wrong with your order, please tell us within 24 hours of delivery with a photo and your order number. |
| Out of stock | I am sorry, [flavour] is sold out today. It can be preordered for [date], or may I suggest [alternative]? |
| Closing | Thank you for choosing Mrs. Samuel. Enjoy your juice. |

Rules for quick replies:
- Keep each under three sentences.
- One fact per reply, so staff can combine them.
- Review them monthly. Prices, hours and delivery rules change.
- When staff type the same answer three times in a week, it should become a quick reply.

### 5. Get the right templates approved

Templates are written in WhatsApp Manager and approved by WhatsApp. Curly brackets are blanks staff fill in when sending. Suggested first set, all text-only:

| Name | Type | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| `order_confirmed` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, thank you for your order {{2}} with Mrs. Samuel Fruit Juice. We are preparing it now. |
| `order_out_for_delivery` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, your order {{2}} is on its way and should reach you by {{3}}. |
| `payment_reminder` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, your order {{2}} is waiting for payment. Reply here if you need any help completing it. |
| `follow_up` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, this is Mrs. Samuel Fruit Juice following up on your recent message. How can we help? |
| `preorder_ready` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, your preorder {{2}} is ready and will be delivered on {{3}}. |
| `feedback_request` | Service update | Hello {{1}}, we hope you enjoyed your order from Mrs. Samuel. We would love to hear what you thought. |

Rules for templates:
- **Service-update templates are for something the customer is already part of:** their order, their payment, their question.
- **Marketing templates** (offers, new flavours) may only go to customers who have agreed to receive offers. They also cost more. Hold these until there is a proper record of who has agreed.
- WhatsApp rejects templates that are vague, shouty or misleading. Write them the way you would speak to a customer at the counter.
- Every template delivered is charged to the business. Do not send one when a reply inside the 24 hours would do.

### 6. Use notes so the business remembers

After any conversation where you learn something lasting, add it to **Notes about this customer**:

- Delivery details: gate code, landmark, "call on arrival".
- Preferences: favourite flavours, no ginger, less sweet.
- Health notes the customer chose to share: allergies, diabetic.
- Relationship: "orders for her office every Friday", "complained about a late delivery on 12 Nov, refunded".

The next person to serve that customer starts ahead. Write notes as facts, politely, as if the customer could read them.

### 7. Hand over properly

A hand-over has three steps, always in this order:

1. **Internal note:** what the customer wants, what has been done, what is needed next.
2. **Assign** to the colleague or team.
3. **Tell the customer** who will follow up and by when.

Use **Pending** for chats waiting on the customer or on stock, so **All open** shows true outstanding work.

### 8. Protect the number

The business number is an asset. WhatsApp rates it **Good**, **Watch** or **Low** from how customers react.

- Never message people who did not contact the business or agree to hear from it.
- If a customer asks to stop, stop, and note it on their contact.
- Do not send the same template to the same customer repeatedly.
- Check the quality rating every morning. If it drops to **Watch**, stop all templates except order updates and tell the owner.

A number rated **Low** can be limited or blocked by WhatsApp, and customers then cannot reach the business.

### 9. Review every week

Fifteen minutes, every Monday, in **Inbox reports** with the last 7 days selected.

| Look at | Ask |
|---|---|
| **New conversations** | Is demand rising or falling? What caused a spike? |
| **Got a reply** | Is it 100%? If not, which chats were missed and why? |
| **Typical first reply** | Are we inside the target? Which day was slowest? |
| **Messages per day** | Which days are busiest? Is the right number of people on? |
| **By staff member** | Is the load fair? Does anyone need help or training? |
| Chats labelled `complaint` | What went wrong, and what one thing would have prevented it? |
| Chats labelled `bulk` | Did every enquiry get a quote and a follow-up? |

Write down one improvement each week and check it the next.

### 10. Turn conversations into sales

- **Answer fast.** The customer who gets a reply in two minutes orders. The one who waits an hour has bought elsewhere.
- **Always end with a next step:** "Shall I place that order for you?" or "Would you like it delivered tomorrow morning?"
- **Offer the alternative** when something is sold out. Never end on "not available".
- **Follow up bulk enquiries** the next day if they go quiet. Use the `bulk` label to find them.
- **Thank and remember regulars.** Label them `vip` and note what they like.
- **Ask for feedback** after delivery, and pass praise to the team.

<a id="rules-to-remember"></a>

## Rules to remember

### The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp lets a business reply freely for 24 hours after a customer's last message. Each new message from the customer restarts the 24 hours.

After that, the only thing you can send is an approved template. When the customer answers it, normal replies work again.

So: reply while the window is open, and do not leave a chat hanging overnight if you can finish it today.

### Templates cost money

WhatsApp charges the business for each template delivered. Use them when there is a reason, not as a habit.

### Only message people who asked

Never send promotions to customers who have not agreed to receive them. If a customer says stop, stop, and add a note on their contact. Customers who block or report the business lower the number's quality rating.

### Keep it professional

- Customers see the business, not you personally. Write as the business.
- Internal notes are for facts the next colleague needs. Write them as if the customer might one day read them.
- Never ask for, or type, card numbers, PINs or passwords in a chat.

### Privacy

- Notes, messages and recordings are for serving the customer. Do not share them outside the team.
- If a customer asks for their data to be deleted, tell the owner. Only the owner can do it.
- Do not export contacts unless the owner has asked you to.

<a id="if-something-goes-wrong"></a>

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A new message has not appeared | Wait a few seconds; the screen updates by itself. If not, click **Refresh app**. |
| **Reply window closed** | More than 24 hours have passed. Click **Send a template**. |
| A red **Failed** under a message | Read the reason shown. Fix it and send again. If it mentions the reply window, send a template. |
| An amber **Not confirmed** | Do not resend straight away. Check whether the customer received it, or wait a minute for the ticks. |
| A template fails with a message about a picture or header | That template needs a picture, which the Inbox cannot attach yet. Choose a text-only template. |
| You cannot find a conversation | Look under **All open** and **Closed**, and clear the **All numbers** and **All labels** filters. |
| You cannot see a conversation a colleague mentioned | It may belong to another team. Ask them to move it to your team or assign it to you. |
| **Customer messages** is missing from the menu | Click **Refresh app**. If still missing, ask the owner for Inbox access. |
| A number shows a status other than **Connected** | Tell the owner. Customers may not be able to reach the business on WhatsApp. |
| The same customer appears twice | Tell the owner, who can merge the two contacts. |
| Nobody on the team can send anything | Tell the owner straight away. The connection to WhatsApp may need renewing. |
