# Lagos area pages: what we need from you

Every Lagos area you deliver to can have its own page, like
`mrssamuel.com/juice-delivery-lagos/lekki`. These pages rank for searches such as
"fresh juice Lekki" and "juice delivery Yaba".

All 14 pages are live, each written for its own area. **What will push them above
competitors is real facts only Mrs. Samuel knows about each area.** Never let two
pages read the same: Google penalises copy-paste pages, and that can hurt the whole site.

## For each area, answer these (a voice note is fine)

1. (Don't send fees. Checkout prices delivery live for each address.)
2. **How fast?** For example "orders before 10am arrive by 2pm".
3. **Real local facts.** At least one, and more is better:
   - estates or streets we deliver to regularly
   - offices, gyms, schools or churches that order (only ones happy to be named)
   - events we've supplied there (a wedding at a known venue, a company end-of-year party)
   - anything special: gate access, a pickup point, a regular weekly drop
4. **Any photos** of deliveries or events in that area.
5. **Customer reviews** from that area, if you have any.

When the answers come in, the developer adds them to that area's page.
