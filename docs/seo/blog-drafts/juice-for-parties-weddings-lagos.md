## How many bottles per guest?

This is the question we're asked most. There's no perfect formula, because every crowd is different, but these rules of thumb are a sensible starting point:

- **Short events (2–3 hours), such as a birthday or naming ceremony:** plan around 1 bottle per guest.
- **Long events (4 hours or more), such as a wedding reception or owambe:** plan around 1.5 to 2 bottles per guest, especially in the hot season.
- **If juice sits alongside soft drinks, water and alcohol:** you can lean towards the lower end.
- **If juice is the main non-alcoholic drink:** lean towards the higher end.

Remember to count the people who aren't on the guest list but will definitely be drinking: the vendors, the band, the photographers, the ushers and the family members helping out back.

We bottle in 650ml, and some flavours also come in 330ml. For most parties, smaller bottles are easier: guests can carry one around, and fewer half-finished bottles end up abandoned on tables. ## Keeping juice cold in Lagos heat

Cold-pressed juice has no preservatives, so temperature matters. Our juices are pressed in the morning and delivered cold. They should be kept refrigerated at 2–6°C and drunk within 48 hours. At an event, this is what keeps them fresh and tasting their best:

1. **Plan your cold storage before delivery day.** Fridges, chest freezers set to chill, or large coolers all work. Make sure there's enough space for the full order.
2. **Use plenty of ice.** Coolers packed with ice blocks or bags of ice keep bottles cold for hours. Top up as the day goes on.
3. **Keep bottles out of direct sun.** A cooler left beside the canopy in the afternoon sun warms up fast. Shade makes a big difference.
4. **Bring bottles out in batches.** Instead of putting everything out at once, serve from the cooler in rounds so each bottle reaches the guest cold.
5. **Don't freeze them solid.** Frozen bottles take a long time to thaw and the texture changes. Chilled is what you want.
